## Git Workflow

- **Trunk-based development** — everyone works off main/trunk.
- All changes go through a **Pull Request**.
- **Two approvals** are required before merging.

Your buddy (assigned in your first week) will walk you through this in more detail during Days 2–5.

---

## What You Get on Day 1

- **Laptop**
- **YubiKey**
- **Credentials** for: email, Slack, GitHub, and staging (staging access is read-only)
- Your email will follow the format `firstname.lastname@bts-synthetic.example`
- GitHub org: `bts-synthetic`

**No production access on Day 1.** The earliest you can get read-only prod access is after 3 working days (requires completing the Prod Access Foundations cert + an IAM request).
