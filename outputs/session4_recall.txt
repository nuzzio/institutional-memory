## 2. Production Access Policy

**Current policy effective: 2026-05-15** (supersedes January 2026 version). Owned by Maya Singh and Carlos Mendes. Triggered by April 2026 incident review (PROD-INC-04-2026) — the old pairing model didn't scale at 280 engineers.

### Three tiers:

| Level | Eligibility | What changed? |
|---|---|---|
| **Read-only** | 3 working days tenure + Prod Access Foundations cert | **Significantly changed** |
| **Read-write** | 6 weeks + read-only + tech lead sign-off + SRE sign-off | Unchanged |
| **Privileged** | ~12 weeks + on-call certification | Unchanged |

### New read-only process (post May 2026):
1. Complete **Prod Access Foundations** course in **BTS Learning portal** (~90 min, self-paced)
2. Pass the end-of-course assessment
3. Get **manager sign-off once** (for the initial certification only)
4. Request access via **IAM platform** (link in completion email)
5. Access is **just-in-time, 4-hour windows** — re-request as needed
6. Manager is auto-notified of each request (no further approval action needed)

**No SRE pairing session. No Slack ticket.** (Both were requirements under the old process.)

### Migration cutoff:
Engineers with read-only access under the old policy must complete the new certification by **2026-06-30** or lose access.
