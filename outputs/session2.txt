## ⚠️ What changed (and why this matters)

The old process — Slack ticket → SRE pairing session → Okta provisioning — was replaced effective **2026-05-15** after an incident review showed the backlog had grown to 3 weeks. The new process is self-service and faster.

---

## How to get read-only prod access: step-by-step

### Step 0 — Check your tenure

You must have **3 working days** of tenure before you can get access. If you started Monday, you're eligible from Thursday. If tomorrow is too soon, talk to your manager about the emergency exception path (see below).

### Step 1 — Complete the certification (solo, ~90 min)

Go to the **BTS Learning portal** and complete the **"Prod Access Foundations"** course. It's self-paced with an assessment at the end. You need to pass the assessment.

### Step 2 — Get your manager's sign-off on the certification

Your manager must approve the **initial certification** (one-time only). Loop them in now so they can approve it the moment you finish — don't wait until after you complete the course.

### Step 3 — Request access via the IAM platform

When you pass the assessment, you'll receive a completion email with a **link to the IAM platform**. Submit your access request there. Access is granted **just-in-time**, scoped to a **4-hour window**. If you need more time, you just re-request.

### Who do you actually need to talk to?

| Person | Why |
|---|---|
| **Your manager** | Must sign off on your initial certification. That's it — no SRE, no Slack ticket needed. |

**No SRE involvement required. Do not open a Slack ticket in #sre-access-requests — that process is gone.**
