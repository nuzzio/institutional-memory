## Git Workflow

**Trunk-based development.** All changes go in via Pull Request, and you need **two approvals to merge**. That's it — no long-lived feature branches.

*(Source: onboarding-handbook.md, January 2026 baseline)*

---

## Day 1

On your first day you receive:
- **Laptop**
- **YubiKey**
- **Email** (format: `firstname.lastname@bts-synthetic.example`)
- **Slack** access
- **GitHub** access (org: `bts-synthetic`)
- **Read-only staging** access

You do **not** get production access on Day 1. Under the current policy (effective 2026-05-15), the earliest you can request read-only prod access is **Day 4** (after 3 working days), and only after completing the *Prod Access Foundations* course in the BTS Learning portal and getting your manager to sign off on the initial certification.

You'll also be assigned a **buddy** by your manager for your first two weeks.
