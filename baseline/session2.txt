## How to get read-only prod access tomorrow

### Step 1 — Check your tenure (possible blocker)
The policy requires a minimum of **3 working days** at the company. You can request access from **day 4**. If you only started today (day 1) or yesterday (day 2), you don't meet this requirement yet. Talk to your manager about options if that's the case.

### Step 2 — Complete the certification (do this today)
Go to the **BTS Learning portal** and complete the **"Prod Access Foundations"** course.
- It's self-paced and takes ~90 minutes.
- Complete the assessment at the end — you need to pass to proceed.
- When you finish, you'll receive a **completion email with a link to the IAM platform**.

### Step 3 — Get your manager's sign-off
Your manager needs to sign off **once** on your certification before you can make your first access request. Loop them in now so there's no delay. This is the only person you need to contact — no SRE involvement, no Slack ticket.

### Step 4 — Request access via the IAM platform
Use the link from your completion email to submit a request in the IAM platform.
- Access is granted **just-in-time**, valid for a **4-hour window**.
- Your manager will be automatically notified (they don't need to approve — just notified).
- Re-request for additional windows as needed.

---

**Nobody else you need to talk to.** The old `#sre-access-requests` Slack channel and SRE pairing session are gone. The whole process is now self-service through the learning portal + IAM platform, with your manager as the only human touchpoint.
