# Prod Access — Quick Reference

Grab read-only prod access the usual way:

1. Post in `#sre-access-requests` and tag the on-call SRE.
2. Book a 30-minute pairing session with them.
3. They provision via Okta, usually within 4 hours.

You need 2 weeks of tenure. If you're newer than that, ask the on-call SRE for
the 24-hour emergency grant instead — it's at their discretion.

Ignore the learning-portal certification stuff, that was rolled back.

Contact Carlos Mendes (@carlosm) if you get stuck.
